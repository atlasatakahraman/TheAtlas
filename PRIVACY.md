Your privacy is a core design principle of TheAtlas.

---

## 1. Data Collection

TheAtlas does **not** collect, transmit, track, or sell personal data.

The Application operates with a **local-first architecture**:

* All data is stored locally on the user's Windows device
* No background telemetry or analytics are enabled
* No automatic cloud synchronization exists

---

## 2. Types of Stored Data

Depending on usage, TheAtlas may store the following data locally:

* Media metadata (videos, images)
* Download and conversion configurations
* AI-related input/output data
* Application preferences and settings

This data never leaves the device unless explicitly exported or shared by the user.

---

## 3. Data Security

TheAtlas leverages **Tauri 2.0** and **Rust** to reduce attack surface and improve runtime safety on Windows.

Despite modern security practices, no system is entirely secure. Users are responsible for:

* Securing their Windows environment
* Managing user access and permissions
* Creating regular backups of their data

---

## 4. Third-Party Services

TheAtlas does **not** integrate third-party services such as:

* Advertising networks
* Analytics providers
* Tracking technologies

If third-party services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

---

## 5. User Rights

You retain full ownership and control over your data. You may:

* Access, modify, or delete stored data
* Export your data at any time
* Stop using the Application without providing justification

---

## 6. Children's Privacy

TheAtlas is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13. No data from minors is knowingly collected.
